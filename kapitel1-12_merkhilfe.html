Netzwerk neu A1 – Memory aid: nouns, chapters 1–12

Sorted by article (der · die · das), with English meanings. Based on the Glossar Deutsch–Englisch, pages 1–48.

der = masculine (blue) die = feminine (red) das = neuter (green)
Rules: word endings help you find the article
  • die is almost always right for: -ung (die Zeitung, die Wohnung), -ion (die Information), -in for women's job titles (die Ärztin, die Lehrerin), -heit / -keit / -schaft (die Krankheit, die Mannschaft), and most nouns ending in -e that are feminine (die Pause, die Lage).
  • das for: -chen (das Würstchen, das Mädchen), -lein, -ment (das Dokument), -um (das Museum), -tum, -nis (das Ergebnis), and verbs used as nouns (das Essen, das Leben, das Lernen).
  • der for: days of the week (der Montag), months and seasons, male jobs ending in -er / -ist / -ant (der Lehrer, der Journalist, der Assistent), and many nouns ending in -er, -ig, -ling, -ismus.
  • Tip: learn every word with its article, e.g. "die Nudel", not just "Nudel". Mixed-gender words (der/das Sandwich, die/das Cola) are listed in the notes at the end of each chapter.
